Privacy policy

There is no use of tracking and analysis tools, cookies, or social network plugins.

Server logs are disabled by default. Because uberspace is awesome.

Query box

When you send a question or a role description, the text goes to Opper, which forwards it to the Clef model on an EU server operated by Melious (Finland) with zero data retention. Opper may keep request traces for operating its service.

To phrase an answer, only my own facts, never your input, go through Opper to Mistral's Mistral Small language model in the EU. "Read aloud" sends that answer to Mistral's Voxtral speech model, also in the EU.

To limit abuse, this site counts requests per visitor for one day. It stores only a keyed hash of your IP address in memory, never the address itself or your text, and forgets it after the day ends.
